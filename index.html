Single Sideband (SSB) & Hilbert Transform ($\hat{m}(t)$) Mechanics

To construct SSB waveforms without complex filtering, the Hilbert Transform generates a 90° ($\pi/2$) phase-shifted version $\hat{m}(t)$ of the modulating audio. Combining the in-phase product $m(t)\cos(\omega_c t)$ and quadrature product $\hat{m}(t)\sin(\omega_c t)$ results in constructive interference for one sideband and complete destructive cancellation for the other.
